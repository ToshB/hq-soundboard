SOUND FORMAT

Given that some platforms (iOS and Android) have trouble playing the OGG sound formats used in previous versions of this sound board, I have created an alternative version that uses MP3 sounds and music instead. This drastically increases file size, sadly, but should hopefully allow use on mobile devices that cannot play OGG sound. Both downloads are offered; just pick the one your device supports (if unsure, MP3 is likely to be more compatible). Note that the version info onscreen will remind you which file format you are using.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDING CUSTOM SOUNDS

You can easily add up to five custom sound effects as follows:
1) Copy up to five MP3 sound files to the subfolder "custom".
2) Rename them "sound_custom1.mp3", "sound_custom2.mp3", "sound_custom3.mp3", "sound_custom4.mp3" and "sound_custom5.mp3".
3) Press the buttons "Custom 1" to "Custom 5" on the sound board to play your custom sound effects.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDING CUSTOM MUSIC

You can easily add two custom music tracks as follows:
1) Copy one or two MP3 music files to the subfolder "custom".
2) Rename them "music_custom1.mp3" and "music_custom2.mp3".
3) Press the buttons "MP3 #1" and "MP3 #2" on the sound board to play your custom music.
